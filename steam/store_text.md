### English
```
[h2]In a World Called a Game[/h2]
There is a world called a game. Its heart, the [b]Main Loop[/b], draws, moves and remembers the world 60 times a second.
But someone built it in a hurry and broke it. The textures are gone, the buildings are copy-pasted, and the physics have stopped working. Out of the cracks crawl the [b]bugs[/b].
You are the [b]Patch Squad[/b], a bug-repair team. Every bug you patch brings color flooding back into the world.

[h2]Shoot, Hide, Patch[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Hold[/b] to pop up from cover and fire. [b]Release[/b] to duck down and reload.
[*]Rusher bugs will bite you even behind cover. Stop them before they reach you.
[*]Shooter bugs flash a red warning circle before they throw. Hide in time and your cover takes the hit.
[*]Piercing missiles go straight through cover. Shoot them down.
[/list]

[h2]A Squad of Three[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Dot[/b] — Rapid fire. The calm squad leader. "If a bug can be reproduced, it can be fixed."
[*][b]Rex[/b] — Spread shot. Seven pellets that knock charging bugs back. "Just refactor it all!"
[*][b]Pin[/b] — Sniper. Charge up to pierce lined-up enemies and armored shells.
[/list]
Every bug has a weak weapon. Swap squad members to match the fight. In a pinch, use the squad skill [b]Rollback[/b] to slow time for 3 seconds and rewind incoming attacks.

[h2]Don't Let It Crash[/h2]
Each hit lowers [b]Stability[/b], and the screen starts to tear, invert and pixelate. Hit 0% and it's a [b]CRASH![/b]
Patch bugs in a row to repair glitches, and shoot a floating [b]Hotfix[/b] to recover stability.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Free Ops (NEW)[/b] — a first-person mode: roam the 3D world with WASD and fight with three unique skills per squad member (Shift, E and a Q ultimate)
[*]A story intro and ending, plus a line from the squad at every stage
[*]4 stages plus a 3-phase boss, "Main Loop": the Textureless Forest, Copy-Paste City, Render Subway and the Broken Physics Zone
[*]7 distinct bug types, piercing missiles and hotfix items, with tips and weaknesses in the Bug Dex
[*]Easy, Normal and Hard difficulty (Hard unlocks after clearing Normal), plus a [b]Daily Build[/b] that changes every day
[*]A hands-on tutorial training ground
[*]Result grades and a shareable, copyable patch note
[*]9 Steam Achievements and Steam Cloud saves
[*]Mouse sensitivity, colorblind mode, reduced flashing and shake, separate SFX and music volume
[/list]
[i]Available in English, Korean, Japanese and Simplified Chinese (switch anytime with the 언어/Language button on the title screen).[/i]
```
